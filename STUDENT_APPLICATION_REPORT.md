## Specific Enhancement Recommendations

### **Replace These Generic Examples:**

#### ❌ **Iris Flower Dataset** (Session 7.1)
**Why it's bad:** No teenager cares about classifying flowers
**Replace with:**
- **Music Genre Classification:** Classify songs into Pop, Hip-Hop, Rock, EDM
  - Features: tempo, energy, danceability, acousticness
  - Goal: Build your own Spotify-like recommender
  - Students can test on their own playlists!

#### ❌ **House Price Prediction** (Session 2.1)
**Why it's bad:** Students aren't buying houses
**Replace with:**
- **College Acceptance Prediction:**
  - Features: GPA, SAT/ACT, extracurriculars, essays
  - Output: Acceptance likelihood
  - Students can estimate their own chances!

#### ❌ **Customer Segmentation** (Session 2.2)
**Why it's bad:** Corporate marketing isn't relatable
**Replace with:**
- **Friend Group Clustering:**
  - Data: Social media connections, shared interests
  - Goal: Find natural friend groups
  - Visualize your own social network!

#### ❌ **Generic Student Scores** (Session 6.2)
**Why it's bad:** Boring, no emotional connection
**Replace with:**
- **Instagram Engagement Optimizer:**
  - Features: Post time, hashtags, filters used, caption length
  - Create: Engagement rate = (Likes + Comments) / Followers
  - Goal: Predict which posts will perform best
  - Students want to optimize their actual posts!
